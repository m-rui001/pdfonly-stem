# 聚乙烯类材料的闭环回收

## Closed-loop recycling of polyethylene-like materials

*Nature* 2021, DOI: 10.1038/s41586-020-03149-9

塑料是现代技术的关键组成部分，但其生产消耗大量原料资源，而使用后大部分被丢弃。就循环经济而言，消费后分类聚合物的理想再利用（“机械回收”）因材料性能劣化而受阻；通过解聚为单体的化学回收则提供了保留高性能的替代途径。聚乙烯的线性烃链可实现结晶堆积并提供优异材料性能，但其惰性本质阻碍化学回收，需要 > 600 °C 的温度且乙烯回收率 < 10%。本文展示，具有低密度链内官能团作为聚乙烯链断点的可再生聚碳酸酯和聚酯，可通过溶剂解以 > 96% 的回收率进行化学回收；同时断点不破坏结晶聚乙烯结构，HDPE 类材料性能在回收后完全保留。加工可通过常规注塑进行，材料也适用于增材制造。从模型聚合物废物流中选择性分离是可能的。原始聚合物由长链结构单元缩聚而成，这些单元通过最先进的催化方案从常见植物油原料或微藻油衍生。该方法（Extended Data Fig. 1）实现了聚乙烯类材料的闭环回收。

## 聚乙烯类聚碳酸酯-18 和聚酯-18,18 的合成与性能

以商业可得的可再生 1,18-十八烷二酸为起始原料，该原料通过烯烃复分解技术从植物油大规模生物精炼生产。通过成熟技术进行酯化和还原，分别得到相应的长链 C18 二甲酯和 C18 二醇。聚酯-18,18（PE-18,18）和聚碳酸酯-18（PC-18）样品此前已基于其他原料制备，但实现聚乙烯类机械性能的关键挑战尚未解决。在我们的工作中，使用碳酸二乙酯（DEC）作为共试剂对 PC-18 达到极高分子量（高达 Mw ~ 300,000 g mol⁻¹，Mn ~ 90,000 g mol⁻¹）具有决定性作用，从而提供延展性和机械强度（Fig. 1a, d-e）。DEC 衍生端基的额外反应路径促进了聚合过程中分子量的增长，而常用的碳酸二甲酯试剂则不具备这一途径（Extended Data Fig. 2）。具有理想机械性能的 PE-18,18 可通过钛醇盐缩聚化学计量的生物精炼来源 C18 二酯和二醇获得（通常 Mw ~ 80,000 g mol⁻¹，Mn ~ 50,000 g mol⁻¹）。两种材料在分子量（Fig. 1c）、模量和延展性方面与商业 HDPE 相当，这些通过注塑成型的试样进行应力-应变实验确定，注塑是 HDPE 的广泛热塑性加工方法（Fig. 1d,e；HDPE 的其他文献比较值为 Ey=900 MPa，σy=27 MPa，LDPE 为 Ey=240 MPa，σy=12 MPa）。

广角 X 射线散射（WAXS）衍射图与 HDPE 几乎相同（Fig. 1b），表明固态结构完全由排列的烃段结晶主导（Fig. 1f）。通过 WAXS 数据解卷积确定的结晶度与 HDPE 基本相同，χ ≈ 80%。如预期，由于链内官能团掺入结晶片层带来的能量惩罚，PC-18 和 PE-18,18 的熔点和结晶点相比 HDPE 有所降低（Extended Data Fig. 3）。尽管如此，结晶温度足够高，便于熔融加工，这对塑料应用至关重要。

## 加工与熔融长丝制造

如前所述，样品测试条通过标准熔融加工方法制备，采用双螺杆微型混炼机和活塞注塑。作为进一步的高要求加工方法，探索了熔融长丝制造的增材制造。对于 3D 打印技术，完全保留材料高性能的闭环回收尤为理想。对于 PC-18 和 PE-18,18 材料，熔融挤出产生的长丝尺寸精度极佳，在 ±0.02 mm 以内，匹配商业长丝的质量（通常 ≤ 0.05 mm，Fig. 2a-c）。对于含着色剂或碳纤维作为性能增强填料的复合材料，也能生成质量无损的长丝。

PC-18 和 PE-18,18 在典型标准打印表面如聚醚酰亚胺或玻璃上打印良好（不同于 HDPE 需要特殊表面）。这可归因于极性链内基团赋予的与聚合物熔体的改进相容性。注意在固态下，这些聚合物的表面张力和润湿角与聚乙烯无显著差异（参见 Supplementary Information），酯和碳酸酯基团可掺入聚合物片层。这允许使用标准商业 3D 打印机直接打印拉伸测试试样（Fig. 2d）。碳纤维增强 10 wt% 的 PC-18 共混物也可打印，无需苛刻条件。3D 打印测试条表现出优异的机械性能，即 PC-18 的高延展性达 εb=350% 和杨氏模量 ~640 MPa（Fig. 2e）。同样，相同条件下打印的 PE-18,18 试样与注塑试样相当，杨氏模量 ~910 MPa 和 εb ~470%（Extended Data Fig. 4）。除上述拉伸测试条外，还可实现更复杂的 3D 打印，如红色 PE-18,18 的卡扣式智能手机保护壳（Fig. 3a）。这一日常物品是机械要求高的多次使用塑料制品的例子，需要可回收性，此处由于特定智能手机代际的有限寿命。在高温稳定性方面，典型基准是水沸点下的稳定性。虽然 PC-18 和 PE-18,18 的软化点对此类应用太低，但值得注意的是，我们的聚乙烯类材料也为这一目标提供了前景。从催化链倍增芥酸产生的 1,48-四十八烷二醇，通过碳酸二乙酯聚合方法得到高分子量（Mw ~ 90,000 g mol⁻¹，Mn ~ 40,000 g mol⁻¹）韧性 PC-48，具有窄熔融转变 Tm=113 °C（Extended Data Fig. 3）。由彩色 PC-48 3D 打印的薄壁（0.4 mm）杯子在反复填充沸水后保持尺寸和形状（Fig. 3b）。

## 聚乙烯类材料的化学回收

长链聚碳酸酯和聚酯均可在温和条件下于良性溶剂中定量化学回收（Fig. 4a）。对于 PC-18，在碱性 EtOH 溶液中解聚产生长链 1,18-十八烷二醇。作为链断裂的副产物，碳酸二乙酯以化学计量量形成（参见 Supplementary Information）。值得注意的是，碳酸酯部分仅占 PC-18 碳含量的 5%（PC-48 中为 2%），但碳酸二乙酯可重复使用。由于其挥发性，可通过蒸馏从反应混合物中轻松分离和回收。

通过 20.0 g 使用过的 PC-18 拉伸测试条在 250 mL 碱性 EtOH（10 wt% KOH）中的聚合物到聚合物回收，展示了闭环回收。采用 120 °C 的解聚温度，无需进一步措施如强烈搅拌或预先研磨即可完全解聚。通常采用 24 h 反应时间以确保完全转化，但 1 h 甚至更短的反应时间也足以实现 > 99% 的转化率（详情和其他解聚条件参见 Extended Data Fig. 5）。注意，在 150 °C 的 MeOH 中 24 h 内无需催化剂也可实现完全解聚。反应混合物冷却后，长链二醇结晶并通过过滤分离。单次重结晶得到高纯度 1,18-十八烷二醇（> 99%），几乎定量产率（17.91 g，对应 98%）。通过将如此回收的单体再次与碳酸二乙酯反应，可获得 19.25 g 高分子量（Mw ~240,000 g mol⁻¹，Mn ~70,000 g mol⁻¹）PC-18，这转化为 96% 的化学聚合物到聚合物回收率（Fig. 4a）。如预期，该回收 PC-18 保留了其材料和加工质量（Extended Data Fig. 6）。

在实际混合塑料废物流的分选中，分离通过沉浮密度分离或 NIR 分析后的机械分选进行。这两种方法都可能将我们的聚乙烯类聚合物与聚丙烯（PP）和聚乙烯一起纳入聚烯烃流中。因此，在化学回收过程中将我们的聚乙烯类聚合物与这些聚烯烃分离的能力是可取的。为此，将商业 PP（可重复使用热饮杯）、HDPE（溶剂瓶盖）和蓝色 PC-18 的碎片进行上述解聚过程（Fig. 4b 和 Extended Data Fig. 7）。虽然聚乙烯类 PC-18 完全解聚并溶解，但 PP 和 HDPE 碎片以原始未改变状态回收。从滤液结晶后处理中，再次定量回收纯 1,18-十八烷二醇（GC: > 99%）（98% 产率）。鉴于聚对苯二甲酸乙二醇酯（PET）也是废物流中的主要塑料并需回收，从 PET 中回收我们的聚合物是可取的，例如避免任一流的污染。值得注意的是，PC-18 可完全解聚为单体，而 PET（Tm=268 °C）保留，材料可定量分离（Extended Data Fig. 7）。

与长链聚碳酸酯不同，其中解聚产生二醇作为唯一长链化合物，PE-18,18 的解聚产生两种非挥发性长链单体的混合物：二酯和二醇。该混合物不易分离，但可直接再聚合为新的 PE-18,18。PE-18,18 在 MeOH 中的解聚无需催化剂，在 150 °C、40 g 聚合物/L 浓度下 12 h 内（或有催化剂时 2 h）实现完全解聚。此外，溶剂解过程甚至允许从与 HDPE 的熔融共混物中选择性解聚和回收 PE-18,18 组分，保持 HDPE 基质完整，从而便于其分离（Extended Figure 8）。

注意，尽管聚合物可按上述方式解聚，但在典型应用的环境条件下，聚合物不会不利地敏感，且数月内未观察到分解证据（参见 Supplementary Information）。从解聚溶液中真空去除 MeOH 得到 C18 二酯和 C18 二醇的精确 1:1 比例混合物（由 1H-NMR 光谱证明；Supplementary Information）。无需进一步后处理，该混合物用 [Ti(OnBu)4] 催化剂再聚合，得到高分子量 PE-18,18（Extended Data Fig. 9）。如此回收的聚合物的热性能和特别是材料性能与原始 PE-18,18 相当，通过注塑试样和 3D 打印试样的拉伸测试证明（参见 Fig. 4c）。

值得注意的是，化学回收的解聚也可通过水作为反应介质的水解进行，尽管疏水聚合物和单体的水性反应混合物具有非均相性质。特别是，聚碳酸酯 PC-18 在碱性条件（KOH）下 180 °C 完全转化为干净的 1,18-二醇，碳酸酯部分以二氧化碳释放（Extended Data Fig. 5）。

总之，我们充分展示了聚乙烯类材料的闭环回收方法。关键是在聚乙烯链中低密度的断点，使得结晶结构不受影响，从而机械强度等性能不受损害。通过断裂 C-C 键化学回收聚乙烯仅反应能量就需要 ΔH≈3 MJ kg⁻¹ 并加热至 800°C，乙烯的选择性有限。相比之下，本文使用的醇解反应本质上是热中性平衡反应，通过过量溶剂驱动解聚。由于断点密度低，在该解聚/聚合循环中循环的试剂量（此处为醇）很低（例如 PE-18,18 甲醇解中为 11 wt.-%）。

适当的直接聚合方案产生长链聚碳酸酯或聚酯，具有高分子量，反映在理想的材料性能上，即延展性和刚性。长链聚碳酸酯和长链聚酯的解聚在温和条件下（120 °C 在 EtOH 或 MeOH 中）发生，并通过重结晶产生高纯度单体，如 PC-18 和 PE-18,18 详细证明。作为链中的断点，碳酸酯基团特别有吸引力，因为它们产生可结晶的长链二醇和易分离的挥发性碳酸二烷基酯。再聚合产生质量无损的材料，聚合物到聚合物回收效率为 96%。这种定量回收也可从商业聚丙烯和聚乙烯（HDPE）的混合物中选择性实现，这些混合物会出现在废塑料流中，且与 PET 的分离也是可能的。着色剂和碳纤维，即使少量通常也对回收有害，可完全去除。除注塑作为服务众多应用的常见广泛加工方法外，聚乙烯类聚合物也非常适合 3D 打印，作为一种补充制造方法，尤其受益于化学回收。

植物油被用作原始原料，因为其长亚甲基序列是所用长链单体的便利来源。值得注意的是，除了通过烯烃复分解对棕榈油进行商业生物精炼外，酶促 ω-氧化或传统植物油或微藻油的羰基化也提供了获取原始聚合物材料的替代技术基础。目前，长链单体的价格和规模尚不及乙烯，乙烯是化学工业中最便宜的构建块。所展示的方法为适用于高质量应用的全可回收聚乙烯类材料提供了前景。这是超越当前所用技术的资源节约型低排放经济的重要潜在构建块。

METHODS

Materials

All chemicals were used as received without further purification. 1,18-octadecanedioic acid was purchased from Elevance Renewable Sciences Inc. Titanium(IV) n-butoxide (97 %), lithium aluminium hydride (95 %), lithium hydride (95 %), diethyl carbonate (≥ 99 %), potassium hydroxide (≥ 99 %) and methanol (≥ 99.8 %) were purchased from Sigma Aldrich. Ethanol (≥ 99.8 %), xylene (isomeric mixture, ≥ 99 %) and tetrahydrofuran (≥ 99.5 %) were purchased from Carl Roth. 2-Propanol (≥ 99.7 %) was purchased from VWR and toluene (≥ 99.9 %) was purchased from Merck. N,O-Bis(trimethylsilyl)trifluoroacetamide (BSTFA) (98%) was purchased from abcr. High-density polyethylene (HDPE) (analogous to industrial grade Lyondellbasell Alathon H5112) was purchased from Sigma Aldrich. Omnidynamics PLA/ABS colourant masterbatch in red and blue was used for the colouration of the 3D-printing filament. Carbon-fibres from R&G Faserverbundwerkstoffe GmbH were used for the fabrication of reinforced 3D-printing filament. Deuterated solvents for NMR spectroscopy were purchased from Eurisotop and dried over molecular sieves from Riedel de-Haen (0.4 nm).

All manipulations involving air- and/or moisture-sensitive substances were carried out under inert atmosphere using standard Schlenk and glovebox techniques.

Characterisation and processing techniques

Nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectra were recorded on a Bruker Avance III HD 400 spectrometer. Chemical shifts were referenced to the signal of the solvent (residual proton resonances for 1H spectra, carbon resonances for 13C spectra). Mestrenova software by Mestrelab Research S.L. (version 14.0.0) was used for data evaluation.

Infrared spectra (IR) of polymer samples were recorded on a Perkin Elmer Spectrum 100 FT-IR attenuated total reflection spectrometer. Spectrum software by Perkin Elmer (version 6.2.0) was used for data evaluation.

Gas chromatography (GC) was carried out on a Perkin Elmer Clarus 500 instrument with an autosampler and flame ionisation detection (FID) on a Perkin Elmer Elite-5 capillary column (5 % diphenyl, 95 % dimethylpolysiloxane, length: 30 m, inner diameter: 0.25 mm, film thickness: 0.25 μm), using helium as carrier gas at a flow rate of 1.5 mL min⁻¹. A temperature programme of an initial isothermal step of 1 min at 70°C, followed by heating with 15°C min⁻¹ to 250°C, and 5 min isothermal at this temperature was used, with an injector temperature of 300°C and a detector temperature of 320°C. Diols were derivatised by silylation using BSTFA for GC analysis.

Molecular weights of the polymers were determined by high temperature gel permeation chromatography (GPC) in 1,2-dichlorobenzene at 160 °C on a Polymer Char GPC-IR instrument, equipped with PSS Polefin Linear XL columns (3 x 30 cm, additional guard column), an infrared detector (IR5 MCT, concentration signal) and a viscosity detector. A standard flow rate of 0.5 mL min⁻¹ was used. Molecular weights were determined via universal calibration versus narrow polystyrene standards from PSS Polymer Standards (software: PSS WinGPC, version 8.32). Mark-Houwink parameters determined for polystyrene and used in the data analysis are α=0.703 and K = 0.01522 mL g⁻¹. Mark-Houwink parameters of PC-18 and PE-18,18 were determined from a Mark-Houwink plot of intrinsic viscosity vs. elution volume.

Differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) measurements of polymers were carried out on a Netzsch DSC 204 F1 instrument (software: Netzsch Proteus Thermal Analysis, version 6.1.0) with a heating/cooling rate of 10 K min⁻¹. Data reported are from second heating cycles.

Scanning electron microscopy (SEM) images were recorded on a Zeiss Gemini 500 microscope by secondary electron detection (SE2) with an acceleration voltage of 3 kV. Polymer samples were sputtered with a 6 nm platinum layer using a Quorum Q150 sputter coater.

Injection moulded test specimens were prepared using a Xplore IM 5.5 micro injection moulder. The cylinder and mould temperature were set to 180 °C and 60 °C, respectively, and an injection pressure of 16 bar for 10 s and 12 bar for 15 s was applied. Prior to injection moulding, the polymers were homogenised under vacuum in a Xplore MC 5 micro compounder at 180 °C and 50 rpm for 30 min.

3D printing (fused filament fabrication) of tensile test specimens and demonstration objects was conducted using a Prusa i3 MK3 printer.

Tensile tests were performed on a Zwick Z005/1446 Retroline tC II instrument at a crosshead speed of 5 mm min⁻¹ on 3D-printed or injection moulded tensile testing specimens (ISO 527-2, type 5A). The determination of the Young's modulus was conducted at a crosshead speed of 1 mm min⁻¹. Prior to tensile testing, the samples were preconditioned at room temperature. The Zwick Roell testXpert software version 11.0 was used for data evaluation.

Wide angle x-ray scattering (WAXS) diffractograms were recorded on a D8 Discover instrument (Bruker) with a Vantec (Bruker) detector. Polymer crystallinities (χWAXS) were determined from the WAXS patterns as: χWAXS=[Ac(110)+Ac(200)]/[Ac(110)+Ac(200)+Aa] where Ac refers to the integrated area of the Bragg reflections from the orthorhombic PE crystal and Aa to the integrated area of the amorphous halo. A Voigt fit was used.

The surface interfacial tensions of PC-18, PE-18,18 and commercial HDPE were determined by the method of Fowkes on a drop shape analyzer DSA25 by KRUSS.

Error bars shown are standard deviations from three to eight independent measurements (tensile properties) or twenty independent measurements (filament diameters), respectively.

Polymerisation experiments

In a typical polycondensation for PC-18, 1,18-octadecane diol (1.0 equiv.) together with a stirring bar was dried in a three-necked Schlenk tube at 60 °C in vacuo. A cooled condensor flask that allows for monitoring of the volatiles released from the polymerisation mixture was connected, diethyl carbonate (2.5 equiv.) was added in a nitrogen stream and the temperature was raised to 120 °C (stirring at 500 rpm). When a homogenous mixture was obtained, LiH (0.5 mol%) was added as a suspension in diethyl carbonate. Typically, 15-45 min after the addition of LiH condensation of ethanol was observed. When the condensation of ethanol had ceased, vacuum was applied by means of a membrane pump connected to the condensor to continue the oligomerisation in vacuo. The pressure was gradually decreased from 900 mbar to 10 mbar over the course of 1 h while the temperature was increased to 180 °C. In the following polymerisation step, high vacuum (ca. 10⁻² mbar) was applied via the Schlenk line, connected to a rotary vane oil pump. The polycondensation was continued for typically 16 h. To facilitate recovery from the vessel, the polymer melt was diluted with xylene at 120 °C and the polymer was precipitated by pouring the solution into -30 °C 2-propanol, washed with 2-propanol and dried in a vacuum oven at 50 °C to afford PC-18.

The polycondensation to PC-48 was conducted analogous to the procedure for PC-18. 1,48-octadecane diol (1.0 equiv.), synthesised from erucic acid by a reported catalytic procedure, was dried in a three-necked Schlenk tube at 100 °C under vacuum. Diethyl carbonate (5.0 equiv.) and LiH (1.0 mol%) were added. After the oligomerisation step, the polymerisation step was conducted for 72 h at 180 °C under high vacuum. The polymer was diluted with xylene at 160 °C, and worked up as described for PC-18.

For the polyesterification to PE-18,18, the same instrumentation was applied as for polycarbonate-18. 1,18-octadecane diol (1.0 equiv.) and 1,18-dimethyl octadecanedioate (1.0 equiv.) were dried in a three-necked Schlenk tube at 60°C under vacuum. A toluene solution (0.028 M) of titanium(IV) n-butoxide (0.084 mol% vs. monomer) was added and the temperature was raised to 180°C (stirring at 350 rpm). Oligomerisation commenced, and vacuum was gradually applied (900 mbar to 10 mbar) over the course of 6 h. The polymerisation step was conducted at 180°C for typically 18 h. The resulting polymer was dissolved at 160°C in xylene, precipitated in -30°C 2-propanol and worked up as described for PC-18.

Larger-Scale Depolymerisation Experiments

In a 500 mL steel pressure vessel, 20.0 g of melt processed PC-18 were subjected to ethanolysis (250 mL ethanol, 2.0 g KOH) by stirring (500 rpm) for 24 h at 120 °C. The methanolysis of 25.0 g melt processed PE-18,18 (400 mL methanol) was conducted accordingly by stirring for 24 h at 120°C.

Concentration in vacuo of the depolymerisation solution of PC-18, crystallisation at -30 °C and finally recrystallisation from methanol at -30 °C yielded 1,18-octadecane diol in high purity (17.9 g, 98 mol%, GC > 99 %).

Complete removal of methanol from the depolymerisation solution of PE-18.18 under reduced pressure yielded a repolymerisable, stoichiometric 1:1 mixture of the two monomers (27.6 g, 99 mol %). In case of the depolymerisation of coloured PE-18,18, the colourant solidified upon cooling of the depolymerisation mixture and was removed from the monomer solution.

Repolymerisation of the so recovered monomers was conducted as described in the section 'Polymerisation experiments'.

Separation from waste streams

To simulate seperation from a potential waste stream, methanolysis (5 mL methanol, 20 mg KOH) of coloured PC-18 (200 mg) was conducted in the presence of samples of HDPE and polypropylene (PP) at 100 °C for 24 h. The polyolefin samples remained unaltered during the solvolysis process (cf. Extended Data Fig. 7) and were seperated by siphoning off the monomer solution. 1,18-octadecane diol was recovered by crystallisation in high purity (180 mg, 98 mol%, GC > 99 %).

Likewise, PP samples added to the above larger-scale depolymerisation of PE-18,18 at 120 °C in methanol for 24 h remained unaltered.

Analogous to the separation from polyolefins, coloured PC-18 was depolymerised in the presence of polyethylene terephthalate in methanol at 150 °C for 24 h. The solid polyethylene terephthalate sample remained intact (cf. Extended Data Figure 7) and was removed from the monomer solution. Neat 1,18-octadecane diol was recovered by crystallisation.

In the final stages of the polycondensation reaction, condensations of two carbonate endgroups with liberation of ROC(=O)OR are the prevailing reactions.
