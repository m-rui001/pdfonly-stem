Pre-Combustion CO2 Capture

• Removes carbon from the fuel before combustion -Carbon is captured in the form of CO2 -Hydrogen is the resulting fuel

• Well known process used for H2 generation for > 50 years

• Currently used to produce high-purity H2 for chemical and refining uses

SEWGS:

Sorbent development

• LT tests showed the stability of the working capacity as well as shift activity of ALKASORB during 5000 cycles, at a minimal steam to carbon ratio (2 mole/mole). • Hence, it was demonstrated that the SEWGS process does not require a shift catalyst, which brings substantial economic and technical benefits. • ALKASORB captures also H2S along with the CO2 without significant loss of capacity.
